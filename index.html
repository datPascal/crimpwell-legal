Crimpwell

Crimpwell is an iPhone and iPad app for climbers with a sore or injured finger. It checks which injury pattern fits your symptoms, tells you how soon to see a doctor and builds a recovery plan that follows your pain scores. It has no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on your phone.
