Support

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Crimpwell can't give medical advice by email. If your finger stays pale, white or blue once your hand is warm, or looks out of shape, call your local emergency number now: 112 in the EU, 999 in the UK, 911 in the US and Canada.

Contact

Write to pascallindenau@googlemail.com. I read every message and usually answer within two working days. Tell me your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong. Please don't send photos of your hand or medical records.

Crimpwell Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Apple handles the payment, so I never see your card or your Apple Account.

  • Restore Pro on a new phone. Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Crimpwell, tap Restore purchase on the welcome screen or Restore on the paywall. If you already use the app, it's under You, Restore purchases.
  • Redeem a code. Tap Redeem code on the paywall, or Redeem a code in the You tab.
  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your answers, check-ins and plan live only on your phone. To delete them, open the You tab and tap Delete all my data. Deleting the app removes them too. I hold no copy, so there is nothing to delete on my side.

Something about your injury

I can't judge your finger from an email. Crimpwell tells you when to see someone and how soon. If you are unsure, see a doctor, a hand specialist or a physiotherapist who works with climbers.

Crimpwell is not a medical device. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
